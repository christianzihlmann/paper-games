PAPER: When a job has work that can be counted and work that can’t, paying for the counted work pulls effort from the rest. So the right bonus for one task depends on every other task in the job: worth paying for Sam’s sales, not for Ms. Ortiz’s test points, even though the test measures perfectly and she responds to pay. The way out is job design: put the countable work in one job with strong pay, and the rest in another on a salary.
